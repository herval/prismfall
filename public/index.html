How to play

Drag to slide the falling trio. Tap to rotate it.

Flick up to shuffle its colors. Flick down to slam it.

Line up 3+ of a color, across, down or diagonal, or fill a whole row.

Clears charge the TILT meter. When it's full, tip the board ◀ ▶: every gem slides over, and each cascade scores double.

Rare Prism gems wipe out every gem of the color they land on.
